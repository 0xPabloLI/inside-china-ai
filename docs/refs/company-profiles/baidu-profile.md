# Company Profile: Baidu (ERNIE)

> Last updated: 2026-08-08

## Basic Info

- **Full name**: Baidu, Inc. (百度公司)
- **Founded**: 2000
- **Founder**: Robin Li (李彦宏) and Eric Xu (徐勇)
- **Type**: Tech Giant (search engine, AI, cloud)
- **Headquarters**: Beijing, China
- **Listing**: NASDAQ: BIDU / HKEX: 9888
- **Employees**: ~40,000+
- **Core business**: China's dominant search engine, AI, autonomous driving (Apollo), cloud computing

## AI Division

- **Team name**: ERNIE Team
- **Consumer brand**: **文心一言** (ERNIE Bot / Wenxin Yiyan)
- **Enterprise API**: Baidu Cloud (Qianfan / 千帆 platform)
- **Open source strategy**: Primarily closed-source. ERNIE models served through Baidu Cloud API. Limited open-source releases.
- **Commercial model**: API + integrated into Baidu search + enterprise cloud + Samsung partnership (Galaxy S24 China)

## Compute Infrastructure

- **Chips**: Large-scale GPU clusters (not publicly disclosed)
- **Export restrictions**: Subject to US export controls
- **Cloud**: Baidu Cloud (百度智能云) — major Chinese cloud provider
- **Autonomous driving**: Apollo project uses significant compute for perception models

## Notable Events

- **2019**: ERNIE series development began — early mover in Chinese LLM space
- **Mar 2023**: ERNIE Bot launched for invited testing — first major Chinese chatbot
- **Aug 2023**: Public release after regulatory approval — one of first approved chatbots in China
- **Apr 2024**: 200+ million users reported
- **2024**: ERNIE 4.0/4.5 released — improved capabilities
- **Samsung partnership**: Powers AI features for Chinese release of Galaxy S24 smartphones

### Regulatory Compliance

ERNIE Bot operates in China and is subject to censorship regulations:
- Refuses answers to politically sensitive questions
- Approved by Chinese regulators before public release
- One of the first chatbots to receive regulatory approval

Source: [Wikipedia — Ernie Bot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernie_Bot)
