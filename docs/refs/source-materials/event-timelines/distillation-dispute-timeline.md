## Key Actors

- **Anthropic**: Accuser. Published blog post naming three Chinese labs. Their encrypted CoT system was the vulnerability that was exploited.
- **GLM/Zhipu**: Pioneer. First to crack encrypted CoT, publicly shared the method — "paving the road" for the industry.
- **DeepSeek**: Accused of 150K exchanges. "Relatively strongest" among Chinese models. Delayed large-scale distillation.
- **Moonshot/Kimi**: Accused of 3.4M exchanges. Rush-released K3 with pure SFT pipeline. Never responded to accusations. Raised $3.5B despite controversy.
- **MiniMax**: Accused of 13M exchanges (largest). Stock crashed 86%. First to pour test sets into training data.
- **ByteDance/Seed**: NOT accused. Anti-distillation policy since 2023. Zhang Yiming's public directive on Aug 6, 2026.
- **Tencent**: Followed in distillation after GLM shared method. The Information reported Claude fine-tuning.
- **Alibaba/Qwen**: Not initially accused, but BBC reported separate Anthropic accusation in Jun 2026.
- **US Government**: Treasury Secretary Bessent threatened sanctions (Jul 21). White House accused K3 of "cloning US tech" (Jul 22).
