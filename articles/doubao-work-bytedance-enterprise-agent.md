# Doubao Work: ByteDance Launches Enterprise AI Agent

ByteDance has officially launched Doubao Work (豆包工作), a new AI agent product under its Doubao brand, designed for productivity and enterprise work scenarios. The product went live on August 25, 2026, with a launch event scheduled for 7 PM that evening.

## What Are Feishu and Lark?

Feishu (飞书) is ByteDance's enterprise collaboration platform, first developed as an internal tool in 2016 and launched as a commercial product in 2019. Think of it as ByteDance's answer to Slack and Microsoft Teams. It combines messaging, shared calendars, cloud documents, video conferencing, and project management into one app.

Lark is the international version of Feishu, launched in April 2019 with data stored in Singapore (Feishu stores data in Beijing). The two operate independently but share the same core features. Lark was banned in the United States in January 2025 under the same law that targeted TikTok. Feishu surpassed $100 million in annual recurring revenue in 2022, according to TechCrunch.

## What Is Doubao Work?

Doubao Work is positioned as an agent product that goes beyond content generation to completing work. According to the official announcement, it can autonomously break down user goals into tasks, call tools, and sustain complex workflows.

The product integrates deeply with Feishu (known internationally as Lark), ByteDance's enterprise collaboration platform. Users who log in with a Feishu account inherit their enterprise knowledge and work context, enabling the agent to access chat records, documents, meeting notes, and schedules within their permission scope. This integration is also available to Lark users outside China.

## Key Capabilities

**Content Generation and Editing**: Doubao Work handles everyday office tasks including writing plans, organizing meeting notes, drafting reports, creating research summaries, and working with spreadsheets and data analysis. It also generates and edits images, videos, web pages, and applications.

**Point-and-Edit Collaboration**: Users can select any part of a generated document, spreadsheet, presentation, or web page and modify it directly, rather than regenerating the entire output. This reduces compute cost and speeds up task completion.

**Browser and Computer Control**: With user authorization, Doubao Work can operate across software applications, use professional tools to process data, browse the web for information, and fill out forms. For long-running tasks, it can leverage cloud computers to continue execution even when the local machine is turned off. Users can also remotely control their computers via phone.

**Multi-Agent Work Squads**: Users can deploy multiple specialized agents that collaborate on complex tasks. For example, a data research report can involve data, research, and design agents working together.

## The Feishu Connection

The deep Feishu integration is what sets Doubao Work apart. Rather than relying solely on user prompts, the agent draws on real enterprise context from Feishu to understand goals, make judgments, and deliver results.

Content created alongside Doubao Work flows back to Feishu as reusable enterprise knowledge, creating a feedback loop where the agent improves over time as context accumulates.

## Organizational Context

This launch follows a major ByteDance restructuring on July 30, 2026, when Feishu's product team was merged into the Doubao team. The new "Doubao Product Team" is led by Zhao Qi, with Feishu CEO Xie Xin reporting to him. Feishu's sales team was integrated with Volcano Engine, ByteDance's cloud platform.

The restructuring aimed to align ByteDance's AI strategy around "model plus scenario": embedding AI capabilities directly into high-frequency enterprise workflows rather than treating them as standalone products.

## Market Position

Doubao remains China's most used AI chatbot with 382 million monthly active users as of June 2026, according to QuestMobile. However, daily revenue remains under 1 million RMB against an estimated 200 billion RMB in annual AI infrastructure spending.

ByteDance's AI business ARR reached $4 billion as of July 2026, exceeding the combined ARR of all other Chinese model companies. The company faces competition from Tencent's WorkBuddy, which leads the agent office market with 20.97 million monthly visits, and Alibaba's recently launched Qianwen Office.

## Security and Certification

Doubao Work implements a full-chain agent security system covering device access, permission controls, quota management, data encryption, and operation auditing. It strictly inherits Feishu's existing permission framework, with personal and enterprise data isolated. ByteDance claims it is among the first products in China to pass dual certification for office agent capabilities and cloud benchmark testing.

## Availability

The desktop version is available for download from the official website and within the latest Doubao desktop app. Users who download, log in, or upgrade can receive a free 30-day subscription.
